SECTION 1: Clinical Education & Guidelines

Definition of Skin Failure

Skin Failure occurs when the skin and underlying tissue die due to hypoperfusion, severe critical illness, or end-stage systemic organ dysfunction. It represents an internal hypoperfusion event rather than an external mechanical pressure injury (HAPI).

Three Categories of Non-Pressure Skin Failure
  • Acute Skin Failure: Occurs concurrently with acute critical illness, hemodynamic collapse, sepsis, or multi-organ failure. Potential for recovery if underlying illness resolves.
  • Chronic Skin Failure: Occurs secondary to chronic end-stage systemic disease (e.g., ESRD, severe PVD, advanced cirrhosis) where healing capacity is severely compromised.
  • End-Stage / SCALE (Skin Changes at Life's End): Occurs during the active dying process as systemic perfusion collapses. Goal shifts entirely to comfort and dignity.

SECTION 2: Standard of Care (SOC) Stop-Gap Gatekeeper

Verify documented prevention measures implemented at least Q-shift for 72 hours PRIOR to wound onset. This gatekeeper reduces false positives and improper HAPI classification.
